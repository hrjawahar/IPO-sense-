How to use

Using IPO Sense, from IPO announcement to listing day

The app follows the IPO calendar. Use the tabs at the bottom of the screen in this order.

The four tabs

ScoreShould I apply? Enter numbers, get a verdict. Listing daySell all, half or hold, with exact orders. WatchlistDaily GMP log and your track record. LearnLessons for first-time investors.
  1. New to IPOs

    Read the basics

    Open Learn and read "IPOs in five minutes", "Investor categories" and "How the retail lottery works". About 10 minutes.

    Open Learn →
  2. IPO announced, before it opens

    Score it and save it

    On Score, tap Clear form, then enter the price band, lot size, issue size and fresh-issue share from the RHP. Add the GMP if it's quoted. Tap Save to watchlist.

    Faster: take a screenshot of the IPO Details table on Chittorgarh or the GMP table on Investorgain and tap Read a screenshot in Quick fill. "What to capture from each site" in that card lists exactly which tables to use.

    Open Score →
  3. Bidding days 1–3

    Log the GMP every day

    On Watchlist, tap Log GMP on the IPO and enter today's GMP and total subscription. The small chart shows if the GMP is rising or falling.

    Open Watchlist →
  4. Day 3, before about 3 pm

    Make the apply decision

    Tap Open in scorer on the IPO. Add the latest QIB, NII and retail subscription, anchor quality and analyst calls. Read the verdict, the Watch-outs, and the Will I get shares? card. Set "Fall I can live with" to the loss you'd accept.

    Apply in your broker or UPI app and approve the mandate before 5 pm. This app doesn't place bids.

  5. Evening before listing

    Prepare the listing-day plan

    If you got shares, open Listing day. Opening an IPO from the Watchlist fills the issue price, shares and expected price. Pick your long-term view and read the plan.

    Open Listing day →
  6. Listing day, 9:00–9:45 am

    Enter the real price and follow the plan

    When the pre-open discovered price appears in your broker app, type it into Listing / pre-open price and set this morning's market mood. Place the orders shown, with limit prices and stop-losses.

  7. After listing

    Record what happened

    On Watchlist, tap Record listing and enter the listing price and day-one close. After 5–10 IPOs, Your track record shows how reliable the score is for you.

iPhone: send a page or screenshot to the app

iPhones don't list web apps in the Share menu, but a free Shortcut can do the same job. Set it up once in the Shortcuts app (it comes with iOS):

Shortcut 1: IPO page. Sends the whole page from Safari.

  1. Open Shortcuts, tap +, and rename it IPO page to IPO Sense.
  2. Tap the ⓘ button, turn on Show in Share Sheet, and under input types keep only Safari web pages and Text.
  3. Add these actions in order: Get Text from Input, then URL Encode.
  4. Add a Text action. Type https://hrjawahar.github.io/IPO-sense-/#fill= and, right after the =, insert the URL Encoded Text variable.
  5. Add Open URLs. Tap Done.

Shortcut 2: screenshot. Same steps, named Screenshot to IPO Sense, with input type Images, and Extract Text from Image in place of Get Text from Input.

To use it: on an IPO page in Safari (Chittorgarh, Investorgain, NSE, BSE or a news article), tap Share → IPO page to IPO Sense. For a screenshot, tap Share → Screenshot to IPO Sense. IPO Sense opens in Safari with the form filled; check the numbers.

Keep using the app in Safari. On iPhone, the home-screen version and Safari keep separate watchlists, and Shortcuts open Safari. Bookmark the app in Safari, or move your list with Export backup and Restore from file.

Reading the score

75–100Strong apply
60–74Apply for listing gains
45–59Only if you'd hold it a year
0–44Skip

A negative GMP, or institutions not filling their quota at close, means Skip whatever the score. "Key data %" at the top of the slip shows how much of the important information you've filled in. Below 50%, treat the verdict as rough.

Quick fill

Optional

Fill the form from a screenshot or copied text of an IPO page instead of typing. Do one table at a time; each fill adds to what's already in the form.

What to capture from each site (Chittorgarh, Investorgain, NSE, BSE, news)

Chittorgarh. Search "company name IPO chittorgarh" and open the IPO page.

  1. IPO Details table (price band, lot size, total issue size, fresh issue, offer for sale). Screenshot it.
  2. Subscription Status table (QIB, NII, Retail, Total). Screenshot it after 5 pm each bidding day; the final-day table is the one that matters.

Investorgain. Search "company name IPO GMP investorgain" and open the GMP page.

  1. GMP trend table (date, IPO price, GMP, estimated listing). Include at least 4 rows so the app can read the 3-day trend and the peak.

NSE or BSE. Open the issue's page from "Current issues" (NSE) or "Public Issues" (BSE).

  1. Bid details / subscription table (QIBs, Non Institutional, Retail Individual, Total). This is the official subscription data.

News articles (Moneycontrol, Economic Times, Business Standard and others) also work. The app picks up the price band, issue size, GMP and subscription figures mentioned in the text.

You fill in yourself: anchor investors, profits and price vs peers (from the RHP or analyst notes), the count of analyst recommendations, and the Nifty's 5-day change.

Long rows on the list page? Don't use the big list of all IPOs. Tap the company's name to open its own IPO page, where the details and subscription tables are short and vertical. For a table that doesn't fit, use your phone's scrolling (long) screenshot, or turn the phone sideways.
Share straight to the app (Android). Install IPO Sense first (Chrome menu ⋮ → Install app or Add to Home screen). Then after taking a screenshot, tap Share and choose IPO Sense. The app opens and reads it automatically. Sharing the page link alone won't work, as a link doesn't carry the numbers.
iPhone: copy text from a screenshot. Open the screenshot in Photos, press and hold on the text, tap Select All then Copy. Come back here and tap Paste copied text. iPhone's own text reading is very accurate.
iPhone: send the whole page with a Shortcut. Set up the shortcuts once (steps in the ? guide). Then on any IPO page in Safari, tap Share → IPO page to IPO Sense.
Can't copy text? Some sites block copying. Take a screenshot instead and use Read a screenshot. Crop it to the table for the best result. Reading works on the phone and needs internet only the first time (about 10 MB).

    Listing day plan

    Fill this in during the pre-open session (from about 9:00 to 9:45 am) once the exchange shows the discovered price. Normal trading starts at 10:00 am.

    Your shares

    My long-term view on the company
    Market this morning

    Your money

    Charges are estimates: STT 0.1% on the sale, exchange and SEBI fees, 18% GST on fees, and about ₹16 DP charge per sell day. Tax assumes short-term capital gains at 20% plus 4% cess on the net gain, before any surcharge.

    Rules for the first hour

    • Use limit orders, not market orders. The first minutes swing a lot and market orders can fill far from the price you saw.
    • Place the stop-loss for any shares you keep as soon as you sell the first part. Don't wait to "see how it goes".
    • Mainboard IPOs above ₹250 crore trade within a 20% band of the discovered price on day one. Smaller issues and SME IPOs have tighter circuits, so you may not be able to exit at once if they hit the lower limit.
    • If the price breaks below your stop, exit. Don't move the stop down.

    Watchlist

    Log the GMP and subscription each day, then record how each IPO actually listed. Over time this becomes your own track record of how well the score works.

    Backup
